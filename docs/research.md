## Agworld pricing

Agworld's pricing page (https://www.agworld.com/pricing, and the Australian page https://www.agworld.com/au/pricing/, which shows the same figures) lists grower packages:

| Package | Listed price | Includes, as listed |
|---|---|---|
| Grower Basic | $1795 /year | Connect with your agronomist, compliant application records, labels and SDS library, harvest yields, tank mix calculator, rainfall. Inventory and grain storage as add-ons. |
| Grower Plus | $3195 /year | Basic plus planning and budgeting, gross margin reporting, templates, work orders for staff, NDVI imagery, weather data, financial snapshots, price management. |
| Grower Pro | $3995 /year | Plus plus machinery and weather station integrations, custom reporting, field groups. |
| Enterprise | Get in touch | Not listed. |

The page shows a dollar sign without naming the currency or saying whether GST is included. The page loads the same figures under the Australian, New Zealand and default locales. Agronomist and contractor packages are separate tabs on the same page.

These figures are a modest subscription per operation, well under the lane's usual five-figure filter. The target was queued at 22 of 30 for data shape, buyers and search demand, and built because it was named for this run.

## Record keeping

- NSW EPA compulsory record keeping, Part 4 of the Pesticides Regulation 2017: record within 48 hours, keep 3 years, record who, what, how, when, where, what it was applied to, how much, and wind speed and direction for outdoor spray equipment. https://www.epa.nsw.gov.au/Your-environment/Pesticides/compulsory-record-keeping
- Growsafe record keeping and NZS 8409:2021. https://www.growsafe.co.nz/Growsafe/Growsafe/Rsrc/Record-keeping.aspx
