# Why there is no front end

Agworld is a record system with screens, maps and connections on top. Underneath are ordinary records: fields, crops by season, sprays, recommendations, the chemical shed and harvest loads. This project keeps those records in a database you own and lets you ask for what you want in plain words through Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor.

## What you gain

- **Your own questions.** "Which crops can we not harvest next week?" is one command, not a report builder.
- **Rules that stop the mistake.** A spray without wind and times, above the label rate, from expired stock or bigger than the field is refused. A harvest inside a withholding period is refused.
- **No per-operation subscription.** Everyone who needs to look can look.
- **Your data in your database.** Plain records. Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **A map.** Agworld draws your fields, layers yield and imagery on them, and drops pins. This has field names and areas, no map.
- **A phone in the paddock.** Agworld has a mobile app. Here, records go in wherever your agent runs, so most farms record at the end of the job.
- **Offline capture.** Not here.
- **Connections.** Agworld connects to machinery, weather stations and other farm software. This connects to nothing out of the box.
- **Shared access for your agronomist and contractors** with per-person permissions inside one app.

Enterprise DNA builds these into a custom version when a farm needs them: a phone screen for the spray record, a map, a weather station feed. If your team needs the screens more than the answers, keep Agworld.
